FAQ

Common questions

What file types do you accept?

STL, OBJ, 3MF, and STEP are ideal. Don't have a file? Send a sketch, photos, or measurements and we can design it for you.

How much does a print cost?

It depends on size, material, quantity, and finishing. Send your project through the quote form and we'll give you a clear price up front.

How long does it take?

Our typical turnaround is 48 hours. Large orders are welcome; for bigger runs or jobs that need design work, we'll confirm the timeline with your quote. Need it sooner? Let us know and we'll do our best.

What materials can you print?

All printable FDM materials, including PLA, PLA+, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU (flexible), nylon, polycarbonate, and carbon-fiber or glass-fiber reinforced blends, plus specialty filaments like silk, matte, wood-fill, and glow-in-the-dark. Custom requirements are welcome, such as a specific brand, color match, or heat, UV, or chemical resistance. Just note it in your quote request, and we'll recommend the best fit if you're not sure.

Do you ship?

Yes. We ship across the US, and local pickup can be arranged.
